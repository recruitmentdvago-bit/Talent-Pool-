# Data Analyst

## Role Overview
Own reporting and insight for the retail banking portfolio. Partner with product and
risk teams to turn transactional data into decisions.

## Responsibilities
- Build and maintain Power BI dashboards for daily/monthly MIS
- Write complex SQL against the data warehouse
- Run cohort and funnel analysis, size opportunities, present to leadership

## Requirements
- Bachelors in Computer Science, Statistics, Economics or related
- 3-7 years of hands-on experience in analytics or MIS
- Advanced SQL and Power BI; strong Excel
- Comfortable presenting to senior stakeholders

## Nice to have
- Python for automation, exposure to ETL tooling, Tableau
